University of Wonderwood

Drawn from the game's own map, sprites, seasons and light (design/day-night.md §7). It opens from file://, with no server.

1. The University of Wonderwood

The stair head: the entrance sign, the banner lamps down the flights, the trees on the flanks. Arrow keys or WASD walk Marriott; the W stands at the north end of the walk, past Larch Hill.

2. The four seasons and the snow day

The same frame in each look: the cherry walk on Green Walkway 1, at the foot of the stair. The season picker doesn't apply here; the hour does.

3. The Customs House

At its full 385 px on the quay, with the Esplanade railing, the bus shelter and a lamp, and Dr. Webb for scale. At night the dial is backlit and light washes up the tower; the windows stay dark.

4. The Ballast on a Friday night

Finn plays Fridays from 8 to 11 PM: the house lights go down and a spot lights the stage. The blade sign and the back door light Spruce Street. Try 9 PM.

5. The cast

All 16 at game scale: walking and turning through the 8 directions, then idle, talking, and their work pose; beside each, the 64 × 64 portrait through its expressions.
